Our Philosophy

"Freediving is not just about holding your breath —
it is about finding stillness within yourself."

At Diveline Apnea School, we believe the ocean teaches us more than technique. Every breath-hold is a conversation between you and the sea — a practice of presence, calm, and self-awareness. Our approach is built on safety first, personal guidance, and respect for the water and the life within it.

🛡️
Safety First

Every session is designed with your safety as the absolute priority. Proper technique, buddy systems, and emergency response are built into every level.

🎯
Personal Approach

Small groups and personal attention mean your learning is tailored to your pace, your goals, and your comfort in the water.

🌊
Respect the Sea

We freedive with love and reverence for the ocean and its biodiversity — especially here in the rich waters of Tomini Bay and the Banda Sea.

About Us

DIVE WITHOUT
LIMITS

📍 Luwuk, Sulawesi Tengah / Central Sulawesi

Diveline Apnea School is an AIDA-affiliated freediving school based in Luwuk, Central Sulawesi — one of Indonesia's most pristine and biodiverse waters. We offer safe, structured, and deeply personal freediving education for all levels, from first-timers to advancing divers.

Courses

CHOOSE
YOUR LEVEL

From zero to advanced — all courses follow internationally recognized AIDA International standards.

01
AIDA Level 1
INTRO TO FREEDIVING

A one-day introduction for anyone curious about freediving. No prior experience needed.

  • Basic freediving theory
  • Safety & buddy system
  • Breathing techniques
  • Pool / confined water session
Beginner Friendly
02
AIDA Level 2
CERTIFIED FREEDIVER

2–3 day course: theory, pool & open water. Target: 20m depth, 90s static apnea.

  • Equalization techniques
  • Finning & streamlining
  • Open water in Luwuk
  • Rescue & safety procedures
AIDA Certified
03
AIDA Level 3
ADVANCED FREEDIVER

Target 40m depth and 2:30 static apnea. For divers ready to go deeper.

  • Advanced equalization (Frenzel)
  • Mouthfill technique
  • Deep open water dives
  • LMC / BO response
Top Level
